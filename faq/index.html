About Apollo Advising

Common decisions

Should I sell my rental property or keep it?

It depends on how much equity is tied up, the cash flow after realistic reserves, the taxes a sale would trigger, how much time the property takes, and what you would do with the money instead. We model the keep, sell, and exchange scenarios side by side so you can compare them, and give you the tax questions to confirm with your CPA.

Is a 1031 exchange worth it?

Sometimes. A 1031 exchange can defer capital gains tax when you sell investment real estate and buy like-kind property, but it comes with strict deadlines (45 days to identify replacement property and 180 days to close) and you take on a new property and often new debt. We help you weigh the tradeoffs and timing and prepare questions for your qualified intermediary, CPA, and attorney. We do not act as an intermediary.

Should I convert my Airbnb to a long-term or medium-term rental?

Compare net income after all costs, not gross revenue. Short-term rentals often earn more on paper but carry higher cleaning, furnishing, platform, and management costs, more vacancy risk, and local ordinance risk. We compare short-term, furnished medium-term, and long-term scenarios for your specific property and market.

Can you help me decide whether to refinance or take out a HELOC on a rental?

Yes, at a strategic level. We look at whether a refinance, cash-out refinance, or HELOC is worth exploring given your goals, and what each would mean for cash flow, reserves, and flexibility. Apollo Advising does not originate, broker, or recommend specific loans or lenders.

I'm retired and own rental properties. Can you help me decide what to do with them?

Yes. Many retirees want less work and more predictability. We lay out the options for each property, such as keeping it with professional management, changing strategy, selling, or exchanging, and what each would require, so you can decide with the numbers in front of you.

What we do not do

Is Apollo Advising an investment adviser?

No. Apollo Advising is a real estate and business consulting firm. It does not provide investment, legal, tax, insurance, or lending advice. Do you give advice on Delaware Statutory Trusts (DSTs)?

No. DSTs are securities, and Apollo Advising does not evaluate or recommend DSTs, REITs, tenant-in-common interests, private placements, or syndications. If you are considering a DST as replacement property in a 1031 exchange, investment advice is available only through Nashional Independent Investment Management LLC, a registered investment adviser, under a separate agreement. Contact Denver to confirm whether that is the right fit.

Not through Apollo Advising. If you need insurance or financing, we can refer you to an affiliated firm or you can use any provider you choose. There is never an obligation to use an affiliate.
