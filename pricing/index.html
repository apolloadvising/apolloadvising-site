Billing

Phone and Zoom time is billed in 15-minute increments. Work between sessions, such as builds, research, modeling, and written analysis, is billed at the same rate. Anything expected to take more than an hour is estimated first and not started without your approval.

In person

In-person days within 50 miles of Dallas are scheduled on a free intro call and booked at least one week ahead. A half day is up to 4 hours and a full day is up to 8 hours on site.

Travel

On-site days outside the local area are booked at least two weeks ahead. Airfare, lodging, and ground travel are billed at cost with no markup, and you approve them before anything is booked.

Changes

Cancel or reschedule a phone or Zoom session at least 24 hours ahead and the time goes back to your balance. Missed sessions are charged as used. In-person and travel terms are confirmed in writing when the date is set.

Ownership

Everything built for you is yours. Denver does not hold your data or your tools on his own computers and does not keep copies after a session.
