Common questions

What do AI sessions cover?

Whatever costs you the most time or money right now. Common starting points are the CRM, lead response and follow-up, after-hours calls, reporting on your numbers, and admin work like estimates and proposals. Denver works through it with you, builds it with you, and teaches you to run it.

Who owns what we build?

You do. Tools are built in your accounts, and everything built for you is yours to own. Denver does not hold your data or your tools on his own computers and does not save any of it after a session.

How much does it cost?

$299 per hour by phone or Zoom, or $799 for a three-hour block that you can use within 12 months. In person within 50 miles of Dallas: $1,249 for a half day or $1,999 for a full day, booked at least one week ahead. On site anywhere else: $2,499 per day plus travel and lodging at cost. The first 30-minute introductory call is free.

Is this financial or business advice?

No. AI sessions are training and implementation help with AI tools and workflows. They are not financial, investment, tax, legal, or accounting advice, and not advice on how to run or invest in your business. They are not connected to Denver McClure's other companies, and you are never asked to use any of them.

Is Apollo Advising an investment adviser?

No. Apollo Advising is a real estate and business consulting firm. It does not provide investment, legal, tax, insurance, or lending advice. Investment advice is offered only through Nashional Independent Investment Management LLC, a separate registered investment adviser affiliated with Denver McClure, under its own agreement.
