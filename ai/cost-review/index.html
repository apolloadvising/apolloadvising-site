Common questions

What is the free AI cost review?

A short intake form about your business, your current software and what it costs, and your biggest headaches, followed by a free 30-minute call with Denver McClure. On the call he walks through where you are likely overspending and what AI could take off your plate. There is no obligation.

Is my information shared?

No. Your answers go only to Apollo Advising and are used only to prepare for your call. Apollo Advising does not sell or share them.

Will you promise I will save money?

No. Some businesses find real savings and some are already lean. The review shows you where the opportunities are, if there are any.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a review of your software, tools, and workflows. It is not financial, investment, tax, or accounting advice.
