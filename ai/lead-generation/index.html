Common questions

What does AI lead generation cost?

Denver's time is $299 per hour by Zoom or phone, $799 for a three-hour block, or in person within 50 miles of Dallas at $1,249 for a half day and $1,999 for a full day. The first 30 minutes are free. Software subscriptions are separate and are billed to you directly by each provider.

Will AI replies sound robotic to my customers?

They should not. Replies are written in your company's voice from your own past messages and answers, and anything sensitive is routed to a person. You review the wording before anything goes live.

Do I need a CRM first?

It helps but is not required. If you do not have one, Denver can build a simple CRM with you as part of the same work. See the CRM page.

Is texting leads legal?

Texting and calling leads is regulated, including consent rules for marketing texts and calls. Denver can point out the common requirements, but you are responsible for compliance and should confirm your setup with your own attorney.
