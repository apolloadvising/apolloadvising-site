Common questions

Can AI answer my business phone?

Yes. AI answering services can take calls after hours, gather job details, and route them to the right person. Recording and consent rules vary by state, so confirm your setup with your attorney.

Will this replace my office staff?

It usually frees them for the work that needs a person. Most owners use AI to cover after hours and repetitive tasks, not to replace the team.
