Can you build a custom CRM with AI? Yes. With current AI tools, a small business can build a working CRM around its own sales process in days rather than months: pipeline stages, contact records, reminders, follow-up, and reports. Denver builds it with you in accounts you own, so you are not locked into his systems or a long software contract.

Set up in your accounts, one-on-one, and taught so you can run it yourself.

  • Your pipeline, your stages

    Stages that match how a job or deal really moves, from first call to paid.

    Every customer, call, text, and note in one record your whole team can see.

Common questions

Should I build a CRM or buy one?

If an off-the-shelf CRM fits how you sell, buying is often simpler. Building makes sense when you are paying for features you do not use, or when your process does not fit the templates. Denver will tell you which camp you are in on the free intro call.

Who owns the CRM and the data?

You do. It is built in your accounts, and Denver does not keep copies of your data or tools on his own computers.

How long does it take?

A simple CRM can be working after a half day or full day together, with refinements in later sessions. Larger builds are estimated in advance.

Can my existing CRM be improved instead?

Yes. Many engagements start by cleaning up and automating the CRM a business already has.
