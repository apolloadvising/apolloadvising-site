Financial reporting

Know your numbers without building a spreadsheet: reports that assemble themselves and arrive on a schedule.

Book a free 30-minute callSee pricing

Can AI automate business financial reports? Yes, for operating reports. AI tools can pull figures from accounting software and your CRM, assemble a one-page summary of revenue, receivables, pipeline, and job status, and email it to you on a set schedule. Your CPA still handles tax and accounting. Denver builds the reports with you so you can read and trust every number.

What Denver builds with you

Set up in your accounts, one-on-one, and taught so you can run it yourself.

  • Scheduled owner reports

    A short report in your inbox on the days you choose, with the numbers you actually check.

  • Accounting plus CRM in one view

    Revenue and receivables next to pipeline and close rate, so sales and cash tell one story.

  • Dashboards

    One screen across accounting, CRM, phones, and website traffic.

  • Exception alerts

    A heads-up when something is off: a large unpaid invoice, a slow week, a drop in leads.

  • Plain-language summaries

    A few sentences on what changed and why it matters, written for an owner, not an accountant.

  • Clear sources

    Every figure traced to where it came from, so you can check it.

Common questions

Is this accounting or tax advice?

No. This is reporting built from your own systems. It is not accounting, tax, or financial advice, and your CPA remains your source for those.

Which accounting software works?

Most common small business accounting and CRM platforms can feed a report. Denver will confirm on the intro call what your systems allow.

Who can see the reports?

Only the people you choose. Reports are built and sent from your accounts, and Denver does not keep copies.

How accurate is it?

As accurate as the books it reads. Every report shows its sources, and the first few runs are checked side by side with your accounting software.
