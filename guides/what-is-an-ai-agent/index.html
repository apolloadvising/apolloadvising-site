What is an AI agent, and does my business need one?

AI agents explained for business owners: what they are, how they differ from a chatbot, what they can safely do in a small business, and where to start.

By Denver McClure, founder of Apollo Advising. Updated October 12, 2026.

Short answer. An AI agent is AI software that can take several steps toward a goal using tools you give it access to, such as reading your CRM, checking an inbox, drafting a reply, or updating a record. A chatbot answers a question; an agent works through a task. Most small businesses benefit from agents that monitor and draft, with a person approving the final step.

Agent versus chatbot

A chatbot responds when you ask it something. An agent is given a job, like reviewing yesterday's leads every morning, and uses connected tools to do the steps: look things up, compare, draft, and report back.

What agents do well in a small business

Monitoring is the best starting point: watching the CRM for stalled deals, the inbox for urgent messages, reviews for complaints, or receivables for late invoices, then summarizing what needs attention. Drafting follow-ups and assembling reports come next.

A team of agents

Larger setups split the work into roles. A chief-of-staff agent briefs the owner, while specialist agents handle reporting, follow-up, or monitoring. Each one has written limits on what it can read and do.

Guardrails that matter

Give agents the least access they need, keep a person approving anything sent to customers or money moved, log what they do, and review the setup when tools or staff change. Each AI provider has its own data terms worth reading.

Does your business need one?

If you spend hours each week checking systems for problems or pulling the same numbers together, an agent can probably take that on. If your processes are not written down yet, start there; an agent needs clear rules to follow.

Common questions

Are AI agents safe to use with customer data?

They can be, with care: use providers whose data terms fit your business, limit access to what each agent needs, and keep a person approving customer-facing actions.

Can an AI agent run my business?

No. Agents handle defined tasks within limits. Decisions about your business stay with you.
