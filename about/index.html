About Denver McClure

Founder of Apollo Advising. Business owner. Marine Corps veteran. Texas landlord.

Denver McClure owns and runs multiple businesses, and he runs them on AI every day: lead generation, CRM, websites and follow-up, phones, financial reporting, and a team of AI agents that keeps operations moving.

That is the point of view he brings to every session. Costs are rising and good help is harder to find, and the owners who adapt will outlast the ones who wait. Denver sits down with you as another owner, not a vendor, to find the work AI can take off your plate, build it in your own accounts, and teach you to run it. You own everything that gets built, and he keeps none of your data.

He founded Apollo Advising in 2023 to give rental property owners something he wished he had when he started: an independent person to check the numbers and talk through the decision, paid for time rather than for a transaction. That practice continues alongside the AI work, at the same hourly rate.

Before business and real estate, Denver served in the United States Marine Corps. He built his own rental portfolio in Texas one property at a time, using house hacking, an ADU conversion, and short-term, medium-term, and long-term rental strategies. Along the way he made the same decisions his clients bring to him now: whether to buy, how to run a property, when to refinance, and when to sell.

How sessions work

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute call. If a session makes sense, you book an hour by Zoom, a three-hour block, or an in-person half or full day in Dallas-Fort Worth. You set the agenda. For AI work, Denver builds alongside you and teaches you to run it. For real estate, he brings the numbers, the tradeoffs, and the questions to take to your CPA, attorney, and lender. All decisions stay with you. See pricing.

Other roles

Denver also holds ownership interests in a registered investment adviser, a loan consulting firm, and a roofing company, and refers clients to independent partners for insurance, tax planning, and 1031 exchanges. Apollo Advising is a separate company and does not act on their behalf. AI sessions are not connected to those companies and are not financial or investment advice.
