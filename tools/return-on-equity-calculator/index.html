Short answer. Divide the rental's annual net cash flow by your equity, which is the market value minus what you owe. A rental worth $450,000 with a $150,000 loan that nets $9,000 a year earns 3% on its $300,000 of equity, before appreciation and loan paydown.

How it is calculated

Equity is today's market value minus your loan balance. Net cash flow is a year of rent minus every operating expense, the full mortgage payment, and a reserve for repairs and capital items. Return on equity is the second number divided by the first.

It leaves out appreciation, loan paydown, tax effects, and the cost of selling, so treat a low result as a reason to look closer rather than a verdict. The return on equity guide explains what the number does and does not tell you.
